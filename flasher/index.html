Clawd Desk Buddy

Flash the firmware straight from your browser, then set up WiFi in the same window.

Check your board first

This firmware needs the Waveshare ESP32-C6-Touch-LCD-1.47, the version with touch.

The similar looking ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 without touch is not supported. It uses different pins and the display stays dark.

Install

  1. Connect the board to your computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press the button below and choose the serial port of the board.
  3. Wait until flashing is done, then enter your WiFi name and password when asked.

Your browser cannot talk to USB devices. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

This page has to be opened over HTTPS to use the flasher.

After flashing

Open http://clawd.local on a phone or computer in the same WiFi to change expressions, colors, brightness and the sleep schedule.

Skipped the WiFi step? The buddy opens its own WiFi network Clawd-Setup. Join it and choose your home network there.
